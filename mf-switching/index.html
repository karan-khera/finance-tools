Option 2 · Move to a PMS
A professionally managed portfolio. Higher fees, its own strategy, and yearly tax on churn.
Flat % of your portfolio each year. No profit share, no hurdle.
e.g. 0.4% base + 6% of every return point above 8%.
Assumes the PMS generates extra return over your Regular funds or an advisor-run Direct plan. This is the alpha you're paying for.
How much of the portfolio the manager sells and rebuys each year. Unlike mutual funds, a PMS realises capital gains as it churns — taxed in your hands every year.

How it works: leaving Regular realises long-term gains taxed at 12.5% above the ₹1.25L exemption — a one-time cost on any switch. Regular compounds at your fund return minus commission; the advisor route earns the same fund return (no commission) minus a flat fee. The PMS earns its own return, minus its fee, minus the tax you owe each year on the gains it books as it churns — the drag a mutual fund defers until you finally sell.
